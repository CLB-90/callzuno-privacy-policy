Callzuno — Official Privacy Policy

Google Play Compliant

1. Overview

Callzuno ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how our application collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our services.

2. Data Collection & Permissions

  • Location Data (GPS): Used solely for the optional Family Protection feature to display live GPS coordinates to approved family circle members and trigger SOS emergency broadcasts.
  • Contacts: Used for real-time Caller ID lookups, custom ringtones, and family group invitations. Contacts are never sold or shared with unauthorized third parties.
  • Device Identifiers & Ads: Used by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) for frequency capping and personalized advertising.

3. Zero Call Recording Policy

Callzuno strictly DOES NOT record, monitor, or transmit audio telephone calls. Your voice conversations remain completely private and on-device.

4. Data Security & Storage

All network data transmitted to our backend (Supabase) is encrypted using industry-standard TLS/SSL encryption.

5. Contact Us

For any questions or data deletion requests, contact our privacy team at: support@callzuno.app
